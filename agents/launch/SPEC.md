# Agent 6: Launch / GTM

**Job:** Turn a PRD into a launch package: positioning, messaging by audience, draft release notes, a launch plan and checklist, and sales and support enablement.

**Inputs:** PRD (Markdown, default `outputs/prd_item_1.md`); optional Company Diagnostic report for market and customer context.

**Output:** Markdown package with:
1. Positioning statement
2. Messaging by audience (customers, sales, support, executives)
3. Draft customer-facing release notes
4. Launch plan and checklist (tiering, channels, timing)
5. Sales and support enablement (talk track, FAQ)
6. Launch risks (including communication risks)
7. Run metrics

**Principles:** Claims must be supported by the PRD; no invented benefits, customers or statistics. For billing and pricing changes, plan customer notice periods, grandfathering and support readiness. Marked drafts need human and legal review before sending.

**Success metrics:** Time vs. manual launch prep; a sales rep or support lead could use the enablement section as written; no unsupported claims.
