## Top Themes (ranked)
1. **Billing transparency and proration confusion** (T-101, T-103): SMB customers are surprised by charges and cannot explain amount changes.
2. **Enterprise invoicing needs** (T-102, T-107): cost-center splits and bulk invoice download are manual and slow for finance and AP teams.
3. **Dunning and failed-payment handling** (T-106): account locks immediately with no warning email.
4. **Pricing model flexibility** (T-105): enterprise prospects want usage-based pricing with a committed minimum; only flat tiers exist.
5. **Downgrade and data-retention clarity** (T-104): a customer cancelled because downgrading looked like it would lose their data.

## Evidence by Theme
Each theme is backed by 1 to 2 items from support, interviews, reviews and sales notes. Evidence is thin for themes 3 to 5 (one item each).

## Segments Affected
SMB: themes 1, 3, 5. Enterprise: themes 2, 4.
